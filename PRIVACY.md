# Privacy policy — Language Sticker (מדבקה דיגיטלית), beta

- The sticker works only on a tab where you click it (Chrome "activeTab"). It has no access to other tabs or sites.
- Translation runs on your computer: a built-in dictionary and Chrome's on-device translator. Page text is not sent anywhere.
- No accounts, analytics, ads, tracking or cookies. The extension can connect to one address only, the project's Google Form, and only when you press "Send to project".
- Stored locally only: your language choice, your own corrections, and a count of sites where you peeled the sticker off.
- Mail, messaging and banking sites are never stickered automatically.
- Optional "quick marking": only on a site you approve (Chrome asks you, one site at a time), the sticker remembers where you last right-clicked on that site, so feedback knows the spot. It reads nothing else and sends nothing by itself. You can cancel it in the feedback panel at any time.
- Core users only (people who received a personal join code from the project owner): "on-screen correction" lets you fix a translation where you see it. When you press send, it sends the original text, the old and new translation, your optional reason, the language pair, the page address (unless you untick it), your anonymous user number and your core-member number (for example 07). Your fixes are also kept on your computer, per language pair.
- Feedback is sent only when you press "Send to project". It goes privately to the project owner (a Google Form), together with a random anonymous user number created on your computer (for example U-7F3K) and the sticker version. It includes the spot you marked on the page (that paragraph's text and what the sticker showed on it). The page address, without anything after "?", is included unless you untick the box. No name, e-mail or device data is sent. Do not include personal details.

המדבקה פועלת רק בלשונית שבה לחצת עליה. התרגום נעשה במחשב שלך, ושום טקסט מהדף לא נשלח החוצה. אין חשבונות, מעקב או פרסומות. משוב יוצא רק בלחיצה שלך על "שליחה לפרויקט". הוא נשלח רק לצוות המדבקה, עם מספר משתמש אנונימי.
