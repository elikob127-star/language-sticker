# מדבקה דיגיטלית — LS · Language Sticker

**BETA — test stage.** Things will break. Your feedback is the point.

A browser "sticker" that translates website menus, buttons and labels **on your screen**, without changing the site. Local first: the dictionary and Chrome's built-in translator run on your computer. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you press **"Send to project"**.

## Use
1. Click the sticker icon → **Translate this site**.
2. Hold **Alt** to see the original.
3. Click the green folded corner (bottom-left) to peel it off. It also sleeps after 5 idle minutes.
4. Mail, messaging and banking sites are never stickered automatically.

## Help build the "Wikipedia of dictionaries"
Wrong or missing translation? Right-click the spot on the page → **Improve translation**. A panel opens beside the page and picks up that spot by itself; just say (🎤) or type what is wrong and **the context / meaning** (the same word can mean different things on different sites). Press **Send to project**. It goes privately to the project team, with an anonymous user number. No account needed.
